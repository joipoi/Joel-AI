This is the home of my swedish AI blog

# Me
My name is Joel, I am swedish and 27 years old. I have a background in IT and programming. I recently got into AI and am now reading a lot about it on blogs and on twitter.
I have no experience blogging or being a writer. I will make many mistakes at first but will try to imrpove.
I am also not an AI expert but I read about it every day and consider myself knowledgeable

# Blog Goal
I think AI is moving super fast and in 2027 it will be all anyone talks about and will change the world.
However I don't see a lot of people around me talk about it. I think many are cluesless to what is going on.
I want to use this blog to teach people what is going on in AI and what might happen in the next few years.
I will write it in swedish and will try to make it non-technical so that anybody can understand it.
I think there is already a lot of people writing about AI but, it is often very technical and in english.
As a start this might just be for people I know around me but might grow bigger later.
I am not trying to be a journalist, I don't have to be objective and I can include my emotions and opinions.

# Target Audience
People who are:
- swedish
- Does not know a lot about AI / does not follow the latest news as much as I do
- Has a hard time following techinal things / does not have a IT background
- is somewhat curious about AI and wants to learn more

# Glossary links
Write `[[Term]]` in a post to link a word to the glossary (`posts/AI_Ordbok.md`). Use `[[Term|text to show]]` to show a different word form, e.g. `[[Hallucination|hallucinera]]`.
- The word gets a dotted underline and a small "?". Clicking it shows the explanation in a popup with a link to the glossary.
- Any part of a glossary heading works and case doesn't matter: `[[RSI]]`, `[[compute]]`.
- New terms: add a `## Heading` in `AI_Ordbok.md`. The first paragraph under it becomes the popup text.
- A term that doesn't exist (e.g. a typo) makes the build fail and lists the known terms.
- Don't rename `AI_Ordbok.md`, the name is set in `src/build.js`.
